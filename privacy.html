Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026

This policy covers the Topple History Uploader and Topple History Publisher, two private tools that the owner of the Topple History channels uses to publish videos to their own accounts on YouTube (@topple.history) and Instagram (@topple.history). The tools aren't offered to anyone else.

What the tools access

They access only the Topple History accounts, with the owner's permission. They upload videos with their titles and descriptions, and they read the performance statistics of those videos (for example views, watch time and retention). They don't access the accounts of viewers or any other person.

YouTube API Services

The Topple History Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

How data is stored

Access tokens and the downloaded statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They aren't sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access to the Google account can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and access to the Instagram account in Instagram's settings under "Apps and websites". After access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted, together with the stored statistics, within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact options on the Topple History YouTube channel.
